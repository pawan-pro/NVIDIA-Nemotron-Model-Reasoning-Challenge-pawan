## Root Cause of yesterday's 0.36 regression
We identified a critical failure in yesterday's trace generator mix (v3.1):
- **stripped reasoning**: The trace generation functions in `v3_mix` completely stripped all step-by-step calculations and verification routines (such as RATE, EX1, and EX2 comparisons), replacing them with single-sentence direct answers.
- **banned phrase assertion**: This stripping was done because the original step-by-step traces contained the phrase *"Ignore story text"*, which triggered a strict QA assertion. Instead of changing the phrasing, the entire CoT logic was deleted.
- **loss of reasoning**: Training a reasoning model like Nemotron-3-Nano-30B with assistant-only loss to immediately output direct answers without intermediate steps degraded its ability to generalize, causing the public score to plunge from **0.56** to **0.36**.
