### Tong writeup main lessons

- Category target solve rates from writeup:
  - numeral: 100%
  - unit_conversion: 100%
  - gravity: 100%
  - cipher: 100%
  - bit_manipulation: ~85%
  - equation_numeric_deduce: ~90%
  - cryptarithm: low single digits

### Tinker conversion caveat
Tong noted Tinker adapters are not directly competition-compatible because of:
- key prefix differences: `model` vs `backbone`
- fused expert weights vs per-expert weights
- separate Mamba projections vs single `in_proj`
- `lm_head` LoRA
- conversion requiring unfusing, SVD merge, renaming, and handling training-serving misalignment

Conclusion:
- Do not prioritize Tinker conversion first.
- Copy the data/trace/masking/validation philosophy first.

### Public validation notebook lesson
The adapter validation notebook reports category-level validation metrics and saves mistakes by category.

This is high-value for us because we currently rely too much on public LB score without seeing category failures.

### Public warnings reviewed
A public discussion warned that a “better” solver dataset can score worse if:
- traces are algorithmically correct but not learnable;
- hard categories are oversampled;
- traces cause gradient saturation / forgetting;
- training data is noisy or gold-conditioned in the wrong way.

This supports our current rule:
- do not blindly scale bits or add hard symbolic traces;
- improve learnable trace quality first.
