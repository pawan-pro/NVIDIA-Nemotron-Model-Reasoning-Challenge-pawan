## Public/community learnings from prior session
- Top public leaderboard is around **0.86–0.87**.
- Many teams up to around rank 675 are clustered near 0.86.
- Our 0.56 proves the pipeline works, but medal-level requires a larger recipe shift.
- Tong Hui Kang’s public 0.85 path is the main reference.
- Key lessons from public resources:
  - SFT is enough; RL is not required for the public 0.85-style path.
  - Deterministic CoT design matters.
  - Prompt/completion masking matters.
  - Minimum logprob is a useful trace-quality objective.
  - Bit manipulation is a major differentiator.
  - Cipher, gravity, unit conversion, and numeral should be near-perfect in strong solutions.
  - Solver-correct traces are not automatically learnable by the LLM.
  - Blindly adding hard symbolic traces or oversampling hard categories can hurt score.
  - Tinker adapters are not directly competition-submittable; conversion is complex and risky.
- Practical conclusion:
  - Do not copy Tinker conversion first.
  - Copy the data, masking, trace-quality, and validation philosophy first.
